Research Interest

My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, political economy, and finance, with a particular focus on human capital, labor markets, and household financial decisions.

My current projects examine wartime legacies, political identity, fiscal incentives, and market development in China. I draw on historical records and contemporary microdata.
